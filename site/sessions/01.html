Warm-up

Step Ups · 登台阶

Equipment
Step / box
Sets × reps
3 × 8 each side
Weight
bodyweight
History
Session 1 3 × 8 each side · Session 3 3 × 8 each side

Key cue Put your whole foot on the step and drive through the heel of that top leg to lift yourself up; don't push off with the back foot. Lower slowly, keeping the knee tracking over your 2nd–3rd toes rather than caving in.

Muscles worked

Primary: Quads, Glutes · Secondary: Hamstrings, Calves

Key cue Feet slightly wider than shoulders, toes turned out a little. Sit back as if onto a chair — hips back first, then down — until your thighs are about parallel to the floor. Keep heels planted and chest facing forward, and let your knees follow your toes as you stand up.

Equipment

Sets × reps
3 × 10
Weight
bodyweight
History
Session 1 3 × 10 · Session 3 3 × 10

Key cue Lie on your back with knees bent, heels about one foot-length from your butt. Push through your heels to lift your hips until shoulders, hips and knees form a straight line. Squeeze your glutes at the top for 1 second — don't arch your lower back to push your belly up.

Block 2

Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift · 哑铃罗马尼亚硬拉

Equipment
Dumbbells ×2
Sets × reps
3 × 8
Weight
20-30 lb (9.1-13.6 kg)
History
Session 1 20-30 lb · Session 3 25 lb

Key cue Keep a slight, fixed bend in your knees. Push your hips back and lean forward, sliding the dumbbells down your thighs until they reach mid-shin or you feel a clear stretch in your hamstrings. Keep your back flat the whole time (no rounding), and squeeze your glutes to drive your hips forward as you stand up.

Muscles worked

Primary: Hamstrings, Glutes · Secondary: Lower back, Forearms

Cool-down

Child's Pose · 婴儿式

Equipment
Bodyweight (kneeling)
Sets × reps
1 × not recorded
Weight
bodyweight
History
Session 1 1 × not recorded · Session 3 1 × not recorded
Note
Cool-down stretch the trainer taught; exact session and hold time not recorded

Key cue Sit back on your heels, fold your torso forward with your forehead on the floor and arms reaching straight out in front. Each time you exhale, let your hips sink toward your heels and reach your hands a little further. You should feel the stretch from your armpits down the sides of your back and in your lower back. Common mistake: letting your hips lift off your heels and only reaching with the hands — then your back barely stretches.

Muscles worked

Primary: Lats, Lower back · Secondary: Glutes

Equipment

Sets × reps
1 × not recorded
Weight
bodyweight
History
Session 1 1 × not recorded · Session 3 1 × not recorded
Note
Cool-down stretch the trainer taught; exact session and hold time not recorded

Key cue Stand tall, raise one arm overhead and bend the elbow so your hand drops behind your head. With the other hand, hold that elbow and gently pull it inward and down. You should feel the stretch along the back of the upper arm (triceps) down into the armpit. Common mistake: arching your lower back or pushing your head forward to go deeper — keep your abs braced and your head facing straight ahead.

Muscles worked

Primary: Triceps · Secondary: Lats

Open Book · 开书式

Equipment
Bodyweight (lying on side)
Sets × reps
1 × not recorded
Weight
bodyweight
History
Session 1 1 × not recorded · Session 3 1 × not recorded
Note
Cool-down stretch the trainer taught; exact session and hold time not recorded

Key cue Lie on your side with the bottom leg straight and the top leg bent so its knee rests on the floor; arms straight out in front of your chest, palms together. Sweep the top hand up and over in an arc like opening a book until it reaches the floor on the other side, turning your head and eyes to follow it. Hold 2–3 seconds, then close slowly. You should feel the stretch opening across your chest, upper back and the side of your waist. Common mistake: letting the top knee lift off the floor — then you're twisting your lower back instead of your upper spine, so keep that knee pinned down.

Muscles worked

Primary: Chest, Obliques · Secondary: Front delt, Upper back
